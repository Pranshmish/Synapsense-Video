# Rule: Handwritten Notes Transcription & PDF Assembly

When converting or transcribing handwritten notes, assignments, or exam preparation sheets:
1. Always activate and follow the handwritten-notes-to-pdf skill workflow.
2. Never perform single-pass OCR or guess silently on ambiguous text.
3. Mark any genuine ambiguity explicitly using [unclear: best-guess] and surface the complete flag list to the user.
4. Separate math/formulas into clean LaTeX ($...$ / $$...), preserve diagrams as clean figures or embedded images, and format tables properly.
5. Perform a dedicated self-check verification pass specifically checking numbers, signs (+/-), units, and subscripts.
6. Preserve the student\'s structural organization, section hierarchy, and numbering.
