1. Executive Summary & 30-Second Elevator Pitch

"I engineered a 6-DOF physical-AI robotic manipulator for autonomous spatial manipulation and trajectory execution. Running on an embedded 32-bit MCU under real-time FreeRTOS, the architecture coordinates six high-torque metal-gear digital servos through hardware timers at 50 Hz. Featuring an analytical inverse kinematics solver converging in under 15ms and cubic-spline S-curve interpolation, the system achieves sub-millimeter repeatable convergence with zero cloud latency."

Firmware & Motion Architecture

  • FreeRTOS Task Scheduler: Dedicated 50 Hz motion planning task with microsecond hardware timer determinism.
  • Analytical IK Solver: Closed-form Denavit-Hartenberg (DH) matrix transformation in <15 ms.
  • S-Curve Trajectory Limiting: Cubic Hermite splines to suppress joint inertia and prevent mechanical gear lash.

Hardware & Mechatronics

  • Actuation: 6x High-Torque Metal-Gear digital servos mounted on CNC reinforced aluminum alloy brackets.
  • Power Topology: Dual isolated rails (5V/10A servo bus with transient TVS protection).
  • End-Effector: Dual-gear compliance claw with proportional gripping telemetry.
