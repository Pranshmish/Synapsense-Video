# Engineering Resume & CV Rules

Guidelines for generating high-impact, ATS-optimized engineering resumes for Physical AI, Robotics, Embedded Systems, and Edge AI roles.

## 1. ATS (Applicant Tracking System) Compatibility
- **Single-Column Architecture**: Do not use complex multi-column sidebars or text frames that scramble chronological parsers.
- **Standard Headings**: Use standard section titles:
  - *Summary* or *Objective*
  - *Technical Skills* (Languages, Frameworks, Hardware, Protocols)
  - *Experience* / *Work History*
  - *Key Engineering Projects*
  - *Education*
- **Action-Verb Formula**: Every bullet point must follow the **Google XYZ Formula**:
  > *"Accomplished [X], as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]."*
  - Example: *"Engineered a 6-DOF analytical inverse kinematics solver in C, achieving sub-15ms convergence and eliminating joint oscillations via cubic spline S-curve trajectory profiling."*

## 2. Layout & Spacing
- Target length: Exactly 1 page for entry/mid-level, exactly 2 pages for senior staff.
- Margins: 0.5 in (12.7 mm) to 0.6 in (15.2 mm).
- Body font size: 10 pt with 1.35 line height.
